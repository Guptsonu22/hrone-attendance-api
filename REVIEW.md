## Examined and Decided Was NOT a Defect

1. **`load_dotenv()` behavior:**
   - In `app/main.py`, `load_dotenv()` is called without `override=True`. In `python-dotenv`, the default is `override=False`, meaning that environment variables already set in the operating system or testing runner (such as `MONGO_URI` and `MONGO_DB`) properly take precedence over any `.env` file. This strictly complies with the environment contract.
2. **Grace period cutoff equality:**
   - In Rule R2, a punch-in exactly at 10 minutes (600 seconds) after shift start (e.g. `09:40:00` for a `09:30` shift) has `late_minutes = 0`. The rule specifies "strictly more than 10:00 after shift_start". The cutoff being strictly greater than 600 seconds is therefore correct.
3. **Absence of `_id` and document `id` on Attendance Records:**
   - `DATA_MODEL.md` and `openapi.yaml` explicitly state that attendance documents are addressed by their natural compound key `(emp_code, date)` and that MongoDB `_id` is internal and must never be exposed. Ensuring attendance responses omit `id` aligns directly with the specification.
4. **Collection names:**
   - The collections `employees` and `attendance_logs` in `app/main.py` accurately match `DATA_MODEL.md`.
